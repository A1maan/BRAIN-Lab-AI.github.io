Research film credits

This 24-second concept film combines original model-layer animation with external research visualizations. The external excerpts are illustrative references, not BRAIN Lab results. Footage reuse clearance is pending before publication.

  1. Google Research: diffusion visualization, original 0.65–4.05 seconds. Single panel cropped, border removed, and monochrome display treatment applied.
  2. Meta SAM 2: segmentation demonstration, original 0.85–2.15 seconds. Slowed, cropped, and color graded.
  3. Video Depth Anything researchers: depth prediction, original 1–3.8 seconds. Cropped and remapped to amber.
  4. Brainchop — Mohamed Masoud and Sergey Plis: segmented brain demo, original 14.45–18.25 seconds. Cropped, color graded, and captured mouse pointer removed.
  5. The Human Brain in Color — Toga, Stevens, Cabeen & Stanis, HHMI Beautiful Biology: original 5.2–5.85 and 6–11.6 seconds. Cropped, retimed, and color graded. MRI-derived pathways, not an AI prediction. Licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0.

The model layers and traveling signals are an original stylized schematic, not measured activations. Colors throughout are artistic treatments, not quantitative medical legends. The homepage grain and blur are separate webpage layers; the video itself contains no added grain. The decorative homepage copy of the film has no audio.
